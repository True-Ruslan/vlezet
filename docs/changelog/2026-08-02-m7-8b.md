## Why

M7.8A established a reproducible benchmark and proved that the accepted local recognition path could return no useful geometry. Product-owner review additionally showed a clear dense developer plan returning zero local walls, while optional cloud geometry remained structurally unrelated to the source.

M7.8B improves the first two deterministic recognition stages only:

1. source and architectural-line normalisation;
2. transient wall topology construction.

The private source plan and screenshots were not committed. Repository-owned synthetic and redrawn-anonymised fixtures remain the measurement authority.

## Root cause found

The browser engine previously iterated OpenCV `HoughLinesP` output through `lines.rows`.

In the shipped OpenCV.js representation, detected lines are exposed as a flat `data32S` array containing groups of four coordinates. The old loop therefore consumed only one segment per Hough pass, regardless of how many lines OpenCV detected. Two edge passes produced at most two useful segments, which explains the repeated zero-wall drafts.

M7.8B now reads every complete four-value record from `data32S` and protects this boundary with a source-contract regression test.

## Delivered

### Source normalisation

- grayscale conversion and histogram equalisation;
- bounded strict pass: Gaussian 5×5, Canny 50/150, Hough threshold 50;
- bounded permissive pass: Gaussian 3×3, Canny 25/90, Hough threshold 32;
- direction-independent segment canonicalisation;
- stable duplicate consolidation;
- short-noise and image-frame rejection;
- orthogonal-plan diagonal suppression only when strong horizontal and vertical support exists;
- complete OpenCV matrix cleanup in `finally`.

### Architectural wall evidence

- paired parallel-edge centreline extraction;
- bounded collinear gap bridging for door/window-sized interruptions;
- length/evidence-weighted dominant wall-thickness band;
- primary structural-component selection;
- provisional confidence capped at medium until the later confidence-calibration slice;
- stable geometry-derived candidate identity.

### Transient wall topology

- endpoint snapping;
- bounded extension to nearby perpendicular intersections;
- T-junction and cross-intersection splitting;
- collinear overlap consolidation;
- zero-length fragment suppression;
- deterministic junction and edge IDs;
- connected-component and isolated-edge diagnostics;
- conversion back to ordinary editable `RecognitionWallCandidate[]` without persisting a graph.

### Fail-closed opening boundary

M7.8B may calculate local opening hypotheses for diagnostic continuity, but it does not add them to the Draft. Door/window classification and verified host-wall assignment belong to M7.8C.

This deliberately preserves:

- `unknown-host openings = 0`;
- no unverified gap being presented as a door or window;
- existing optional AI review and manual workflows.

The product shows an informational diagnostic when opening hypotheses are deferred.
