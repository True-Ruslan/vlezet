The private plan and screenshots were not committed.

## Delivered

### Region-first CV

- Otsu binary inversion and bounded morphological opening;
- deterministic extraction of horizontal and vertical filled wall regions;
- thickness, length, overlap and aspect-ratio validation;
- bounded Canny/Hough fallback only when region evidence is insufficient;
- fail-closed review budget for candidate explosions;
- sanitisation of previously persisted overloaded Drafts;
- deterministic transient wall topology.

### Verification-only AI

- exact local candidate IDs and coordinates are sent to the provider;
- unknown IDs and changed coordinates are rejected;
- cloud-only walls, unbounded lines and candidate explosions are rejected;
- AI may change confirmation/confidence evidence only;
- local geometry is never moved or expanded by AI;
- cloud openings remain disabled until M7.8C;
- repeated checks preserve valid decisions and prevent stale references.

### Experimental wall completion decision

Evidence-gated wall completion was implemented as a bounded pure module with regression tests. Product testing showed no visible geometric improvement on the representative plan.

Final decision:

- production completion runtime disabled;
- experimental code and tests retained;
- engine remains version `5`;
- no baseline migration to version `6`;
- accepted runtime remains region-first CV plus verification-only AI.
