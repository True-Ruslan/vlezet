# M7.6 — Geometry and Opening Inspector

**Date:** 2026-08-01  
**PR:** #33  
**Status:** accepted by the product owner; protected squash merge pending final exact-head checks.

## Why

Precise geometry editing was already authoritative, but its presentation still exposed implementation-oriented concepts. Ordinary users could confuse clear room dimensions, wall-axis length and wall thickness; opening offsets depended on hidden directed-wall semantics; and door swing was expressed through internal `start/end` and `left/right` values rather than visible physical choices.

M7.6 resolves `UX-GEO-001`, `UX-GEO-002` and `UX-GEO-003` without changing geometry authority.

## Delivered

- room inspector cards for `По горизонтали` and `По вертикали` clear interior dimensions;
- local validation and one explicit semantic Apply action per room axis;
- wall-axis cue with screen-stable visible endpoints;
- wall-thickness cue with screen-stable physical surfaces;
- correct presentation mapping for forward, reverse-directed and diagonal walls;
- separate opening-width and opening-position sections;
- reversible offset measurement from either visible wall end without moving the opening;
- four accessible door-swing choices using visible hinge side and opening side;
- four distinct runtime-only Canvas door previews;
- runtime-only active-axis emphasis using existing authoritative dimension annotations;
- fail-closed invalid opening drafts that keep the inspector usable and retain last authoritative geometry;
- compact single-column door choices without document-level horizontal overflow;
- one-step semantic Undo for applied room, wall and opening edits.

## Architecture preserved

No changes were made to:

- `VlezetDocument`, schema, migrations or portable backup format;
- IndexedDB project records;
- topology, room derivation or area calculation;
- snapping, hit testing or opening-validation authority;
- editor-core command semantics or history grouping;
- recognition or planning algorithms;
- Canvas geometry authority or read-only Three.js authority.

The new presentation module is framework/store independent. The preview store contains only entity IDs and presentation choices and is never serialized or added to semantic history.
