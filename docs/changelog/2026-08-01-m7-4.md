## Why

M7.1–M7.3 established a reachable editor shell, unified context anatomy and governed components, but Canvas interaction still required users to remember the active tool and infer the next action. Hover, selection, placement preview, invalid targets and cancellation were not communicated through one consistent model.

M7.4 therefore focused on interaction feedback while deliberately preserving geometry, snapping, hit testing, history and persistence authority.

## Delivered

- one authoritative Canvas mode and next-action status;
- active-tool feedback for Select, Wall, Door, Window, Measure, furniture placement, tracing/review and read-only 3D;
- explicit first-point and second-point phases for walls and tape measurement;
- one-level `Escape` priority across object gestures, measurement, wall drafts, placement, tracing, workflows, tools, selection and 3D;
- distinct ordinary, hover, selected, valid-preview and invalid-preview presentation;
- cursor roles for selection, drawing, valid/invalid placement and panning;
- live door/window preview labels and invalid-target feedback;
- equivalent compact-width meaning without document horizontal overflow;
- Chromium full-flow and WebKit core-smoke regression coverage.
